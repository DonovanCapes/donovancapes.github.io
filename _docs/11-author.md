Predictive Politics was built by **Donovan Capes**, based in Victoria, British Columbia.

Donovan built this site as a personal project driven by an interest in Canadian politics and data. The model, code, and visualizations are all original work, developed independently in his spare time. The goal was to create something that goes beyond simple national horse-race polling summaries and shows what poll movements actually imply at the riding level, with honest uncertainty attached.

The source code for the site is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/DonovanCapes).
